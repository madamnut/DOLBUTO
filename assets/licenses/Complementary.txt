Complementary Unbound lighting and water adaptation

Reference supplied in ref/ComplementaryUnbound: Complementary Shaders by EminGT.
Project: https://www.complementary.dev/

The lighting, distorted shadow sampling, sky/fog, volumetric-light colour and
weighting, scene-aware shaft intensity, tone mapping and TAA in shaders/
complementary.glsl, environment.glsl, shadow.frag, scene_factor.frag, tone.frag,
bloom.frag and taa.frag adapt the supplied shader sources to this Vulkan renderer.
Reference noise.png and cloud-water.png are included under assets/textures/effects
with the complementary_ prefix, unchanged from the supplied reference.
Original volumetric tracing credits Robobo1221. Original TAA reprojection and
jitter credit Chocapic13. Original tone mapping credits Timothy Lottes and BSL.

This is a modified integration, not the original Complementary shader pack.
Water material, texture normals/parallax, reflection and refraction in
water_common.glsl, water.frag, water_trace.glsl and atmosphere.frag adapt
lib/materials/specificMaterials/translucents/water.glsl, reflections.glsl,
reflectionBackground.glsl and refraction.glsl. Water GGX in water_highlight.glsl
adapts lib/lighting/ggx.glsl, which credits Horizon Zero Dawn for the area light
approximation. Bayer dithering in the reference credits Jessie.

Cloud density, ray stepping, opacity accumulation, height/directional shading
and distance fading in clouds.glsl adapt lib/atmospherics/clouds/mainClouds.glsl,
unboundClouds.glsl, lib/colors/cloudColors.glsl, lib/lighting/cloudShadows.glsl
and Noise3D from lib/util/commonFunctions.glsl. The original noise.png is reused.

Minecraft/Iris inputs are mapped to this game's world and renderer. The bundled
reference contains no separate license text; this attribution is not a grant of
rights to the original work. Preserve original author attribution.

Procedural sun/moon size, colour and horizon blending in celestial.glsl adapt
program/gbuffers_skybasic.glsl (SUN_MOON_STYLE 2). Star distribution and palette
reference lib/atmospherics/stars.glsl. Moon surface noise and filtered circular
star profiles are local texture-free adaptations; no celestial images are used.

Regular ice in water.frag adapts translucentIPBR.glsl material 32004
(green-channel gloss/highlight and reflection multiplier), with the common
translucent Fresnel from gbuffers_water.glsl. Ice shadow transmission is a
local approximation; no water animation or generated normals are used for ice.
